Important Update Regarding Our Office Relocation & Telemedicine Visits

During this transition, we remain committed to providing uninterrupted care. You can continue to schedule appointments, discuss your sleep concerns, review test results, and receive treatment recommendations through secure telemedicine visits.

As of July 24, all patient appointments will be conducted via telemedicine while we relocate our office to better serve you.

Important Update for Medicare Patients: We are pleased to announce that we can now continue seeing Medicare patients via telemedicine, as the current Medicare telehealth guidelines have been extended through December 2027.

Medicare patients may schedule and complete their appointments through telemedicine during our office relocation, just like our other patients.

Once our relocation is complete, we will announce the reopening of our office and begin scheduling in-person appointments.

We appreciate your patience and understanding during this transition and look forward to continuing to care for your sleep health. If you have any questions or would like to schedule an appointment, please contact our office.
